### 1. Can I sync my appointments with Google or Outlook Calendar?

Definitely! Zoho Bookings' AI scheduling software offers two-way sync for both Google and Outlook Calendar. Your appointments stay updated across platforms to avoid double-bookings and catch scheduling conflicts.

### 2. How is Zoho Bookings different from Calendly or Acuity?

Zoho Bookings is AI scheduling software that stands out with its extensive customization capabilities. It drives end-to-end appointment scheduling from showcasing availability to automated followups. Check out this comprehensive guide to learn more.

### 3. Can I collect payments through Zoho Bookings?

Yes, Zoho Bookings helps you collect payments via Stripe, PayPal, Razorpay, and other major payment providers for a hassle-free customer experience.

### 4. Is the booking system embeddable on my website?

Absolutely! You can easily embed your booking page as a widget on your website. The booking page displays staff details, service or event types, and available slots. Here's a widget guide that shows you how.

### 5. Does Zoho Bookings support time zone conversion and multiple currencies?

Yes, you can set the booking time zone to match the customer's location, your business, or any other preference. Read more about managing time zones.

You'll choose your currency during setup, but the Zoho Bookings team can change it upon request. Write to support@zohobookings.com for assistance.

### 6. Can I try Zoho Bookings for free before purchasing?

Of course! Zoho Bookings provides a 15-day trial period—no credit card details required.

### 7. What's the pricing for Zoho Bookings?

Zoho Bookings provides user-based pricing, so you only pay for the number of staff you add to your Bookings dashboard. Learn about pricing.
