## Frequently asked questions

Bigin is a CRM built for small businesses. It brings leads, deals, customer conversations, and follow-ups into one organized workspace so you stop juggling spreadsheets, inboxes, and chat threads. Over 50,000 businesses run on Bigin across sales, onboarding, operations, customer service, and marketing.

Bigin is built for small businesses with 1 to 20 employees, including agencies, consultancies, real estate firms, schools, clinics, retailers, and ecommerce brands. If you've outgrown spreadsheets but find traditional CRMs too complex or expensive, Bigin sits in that gap.

Zoho CRM is built for mid-market and enterprise sales teams with multi-step workflows and deep customization. Bigin is simpler, costs less, and gets you running in about 30 minutes. You can upgrade to Zoho CRM later if your business grows beyond Bigin's scope.

Bigin has three editions: Express, Premier, and Bigin 360. Each is priced for small business budgets with monthly and annual billing options. See the pricing page for current rates in your region.

The 15-day free trial requires no credit card and no commitment. You can cancel anytime, and your data stays available if you decide to come back later.
