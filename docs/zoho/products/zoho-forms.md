## 7 AI-powered features by Zoho Forms

Zoho Forms includes seven AI-powered features, all managed through a centralized AI Assistant. Here is what each one does we have to offer.

### AI form builder: Generate a complete form from a prompt

Describe the form you need in plain language: "a patient intake form for a physiotherapy clinic" or "an event registration form for a product launch", and the Zoho Forms' AI form builder generates a fully structured live form in seconds. You dont need to worry about fields, structure, and layout, Zia takes care of it for you. Simply review, adjust, and publish.

### AI forms from PDFs: Convert a static PDF into a live, editable form

Have an existing PDF form? Zoho Forms' PDF-to-form conversion is an AI-powered feature that reads an uploaded PDF, detects every field and label, and rebuilds it as a fully editable, mobile-friendly Zoho Form. Businesses transitioning from paper-heavy or legacy PDF processes can digitize existing forms without manually recreating them field by field. Just upload the PDF, review the generated form, and publish.

### Image-to-form conversion: Turn a photo of a form into a digital form

Alongside PDF conversion, Zoho Forms supports image-to-form conversion as a separate AI-powered feature. Upload or photograph any paper form, and the AI identifies the fields, labels, and structure, and converts it into a live, fillable online form.

### Smart Scan: AI-powered field prefill from uploaded images

Add a Smart Scan field to your form, and respondents can upload a photo of a document like a business card, ID, receipt, or insurance card. Zia extracts the information and automatically fills the mapped form fields. Smart Scan uses OCR combined with AI to understand the image, so it can identify and route data to the right fields accurately, easily eliminating manual entry for event check-ins, healthcare intake, expense reporting and more.

### Write better email notification content with AI

Zoho Forms includes an AI-powered feature for generating email notification content directly in the form builder. Give Zia a prompt: "draft a confirmation email for someone who registered for our annual conference", and it generates a professional, context-aware email. Adjust the tone or length before saving and you can have respondent confirmation emails, follow-up messages, and internal notification copy, ready to be sent in seconds.

### Generate and refine Thank You page content with AI

The moment after a respondent clicks Submit is an opportunity to guide and reassure. With Zia, you can generate a clear, purposeful Thank You page message tailored to your form's context—instead of a generic confirmation every respondent sees.

### AI-powered settings search: Find any setting instantly

Not sure where a specific setting lives? Ask. The AI chatbot in Zoho Forms Settings can take you directly to the right option, removing the need to manually search through menus. This builder-side AI-powered feature is designed to reduce setup time for form administrators.

Note: All seven features run on Zia, Zoho's proprietary AI, built on Zoho's own infrastructure, which means your form data and the content you generate stays within Zoho's ecosystem and is never stored or used to train external models.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a form?

A form is a printed or electronic document used for data collection. A form contains numerous spaces or 'fields' in which one can write or enter data. Sharing forms is a user-friendly approach to conducting surveys, collecting information, taking orders, and more. Forms are an essential data collection tool for gathering and organizing data efficiently, whether it’s for lead generation, registrations, applications, or order processing.

Online forms streamline data collection, reduce errors, and ensure that information is organized and accessible, making them vital for informed decision-making in any field. With online forms, especially those built using Zoho Forms, you can automate data capture, tailor questions to your needs, and simplify processes like customer inquiries, feedback collection, lead generation, or employee onboarding.

### What is a form builder?

A form builder helps you streamline data collection with ease and accuracy. Design custom forms, automate workflows, and capture responses. A form builder is an online platform, which is used for form creation. Nowadays, online form builder apps can be accessed through any device, including your mobile phone.

### What is a custom form creator?

A custom form creator is a data collection tool that allows you to design and build forms tailored to your unique requirements. With features like drag-and-drop fields, customizable templates, and conditional logic, you can create forms for any purpose, from customer feedback and surveys to event registrations and order forms.
