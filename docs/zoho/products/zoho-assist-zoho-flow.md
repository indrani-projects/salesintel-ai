# Zoho Flow

## Zoho Assist with Zoho Flow

With Zoho Flow, automate your business workflows by setting up triggers/actions to schedule a remote session, create a remote session, and to update unattended computer details without codes.

## Integrating Zoho Assist with Zoho Flow

- Log in to **Zoho Flow**and create your own automation space by providing your organization name, selecting your time zone, and choosing the preferred date format, then click**Create**.
- Click the **create flow**option to get started with designing your flow.
- Fill in the flow name and description suitable for the type of flow you're creating and choose **Create**.
- Choose the app-based trigger from the available triggers - *App, Schedule & Webhook*and click**Configure**.
- Search and select "**Zoho Assist**" in the app trigger window and continue by pressing**Next**.
- Select the "**New Unattended Computer**" trigger and click**Next**.
- To integrate your **Zoho Assist**into the flow, click**Connect**.
- Configure the details of your connection by specifying the connection name and customizing it with required actions and triggers, then click **Authorize**.
- Click **Done**after filling in the variable name and selecting your department to complete setting up Zoho Assist as a trigger application in Zoho Flow.

## Creating a Remote Session in Zoho Flow

- Once your **Zoho Assist**connection is integrated with**Zoho Flow**, you'll be able to configure lists of tasks as actions using the applications and logic in the**builder**window.
- Search and select "**Zoho Assist**", then navigate to**Apps**in the left side of the builder window to access the list of actions required to start a remote session.
- Drag and drop the **Schedule Session**action from the**Zoho Assist**dropdown to plan and organize a remote session with reminders.
- Click **Done**.
- To start a session, drag and drop the **Create Session**action from the dropdown and fill in the variable name, customer's email address, and choose the session type (remote support or screen sharing) based on your purpose.
- Click **Done**.
- Drag and drop the **Update Unattended Computer**action from the dropdown to update the details of the existing unattended computer.
- Click **Done**.
- Toggle "**the your flow is**" option to make the created flow live.
