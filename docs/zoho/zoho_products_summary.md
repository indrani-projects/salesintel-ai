## Zoho Assist + Zoho Desk
_Zoho Assist integration._
Integrating Zoho Assist with Zoho Desk allows you to initiate remote support or screen sharing sessions, as WELL as unattended access directly from your support tickets. No prior setup or installation is required on your end or your remote customer's end to initiate a session.
- Log in to your Zoho Desk account, then navigate to Viewsin the left menu.
- Open the relevant Desk support ticketand clickRemote Assist.
- Choose either Access Remote ScreenorShare My ScreenunderRemote Supportto initiate the respective session.
- To initiate a session immediately, click Start Now.
- Choose Unattended Accessto access a device remotely.
Source: https://www.zoho.com/assist/help/integrations/zoho-desk.html

## Zoho Assist + Zoho CRM
_Zoho Assist integration._
With the Assist-CRM integration, you can now initiate a remote support or screen sharing session right from Zoho CRM.
- Log in to your Zoho CRM account.
- Select the Settings option from the top-left corner of the screen.
- Navigate to the Marketplace category and choose Zoho.
- Under the list of extensions available below Zoho, find Zoho Assist and click the Setup Now button.
- Agree to all the terms and conditions by enabling the checkbox next to the I have agreed to the Terms of Service option.
Source: https://www.zoho.com/assist/help/integrations/zoho-crm.html

## Zoho Assist + Zoho Bookings
_Zoho Assist integration._
Session scheduling and tracking is now taken to a whole new level with the Zoho Assist – Zoho Bookings integration. ## Zoho Assist + Zoho Flow
_Zoho Assist integration._
With Zoho Flow, automate your business workflows by setting up triggers/actions to schedule a remote session, create a remote session, and to update unattended computer details without codes.
- Click the create flowoption to get started with designing your flow.
- Fill in the flow name and description suitable for the type of flow you're creating and choose Create.
- Choose the app-based trigger from the available triggers - App, Schedule & Webhookand clickConfigure.
- Search and select "Zoho Assist" in the app trigger window and continue by pressingNext.
- Select the "New Unattended Computer" trigger and clickNext.
Source: https://www.zoho.com/assist/help/integrations/zoho-flow.html

## Zoho Assist + Zoho Mail
_Zoho Assist integration._
Utilize Zoho Assist's integration with Zoho Mail's to start remote support or screen sharing sessions right from your Zoho Mail account. You can initiate or schedule a session with an email contact as you read the mail. The integration does not require any configuration as it is enabled by default.
- Login to your Zoho Mail account.
- Click on toggle integration at the bottom-right corner.
- Select Zoho Assist from the extensions menu to start or schedule a session.
- Click on Remote Support or Share My Screen and choose Start Now to initiate a remote support or screen sharing session.
- If no email is open or you want to send the email to a person other than sender, enter the email address in the space and click on Proceed.
Source: https://www.zoho.com/assist/help/integrations/zoho-mail.html
