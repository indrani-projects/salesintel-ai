# Zoho — Product Capability Sheet (Internal Sales Knowledge)
*First-party reference for pre-sales briefs. Facts are public/approximate — adjust as needed.*

## What Zoho is
Zoho is a bootstrapped, profitable Indian software company (HQ: Chennai) offering
45+ integrated business apps. Its flagship bundle, **Zoho One**, gives a company
every app on one platform for a single low per-employee price. Core positioning:
one integrated suite at a fraction of the cost of stitching together
Salesforce + HubSpot + Freshdesk + separate BI tools.

## Products most relevant to a growing D2C / omnichannel retail brand

### Zoho CRM — sales pipeline & unified customer view
- Manages leads, deals, and pipeline for B2B / institutional / bulk sales
- Omnichannel capture: web, phone, email, social, WhatsApp, marketplaces
- Sales automation, forecasting, territory & team management
- Single customer record across online + offline + marketplace channels
- Strong fit: companies whose customer data is scattered across Shopify,
  Amazon/Flipkart, and physical stores, or who run B2B deals on spreadsheets

### Zoho Desk — customer service & post-purchase
- Ticketing across email, chat, phone, social
- Fits high-warranty / trial-and-return businesses (returns, replacements, claims)
- SLAs, self-service knowledge base, agent automation

### Zoho Marketing Automation / Campaigns — retention & cross-sell
- Email/SMS campaigns, segmentation, lifecycle journeys
- Fits low-repeat-frequency categories that must cross-sell accessories
  (e.g. mattress buyer → pillows, bedding, protectors)

### Zoho Analytics — unified reporting / BI
- Blends data across sales, service, marketing, and channels into one dashboard
- Replaces fragmented per-channel reporting

## Why companies choose Zoho (differentiators)
- **Cost**: aggressive per-user / per-employee pricing; frequently replaces
  Salesforce at large annual savings
- **Integrated suite**: apps work together out of the box — no expensive
  system-integration to connect CRM, service, marketing, and BI
- **Data residency**: India data-center option — relevant for Indian brands
  sensitive about where customer data lives
- **Fast implementation & local support**: Indian company, local presence
- **Land-and-expand**: start with CRM, grow into the full Zoho One suite

## Proof points (public references)
- Atlantic Tactical replaced Salesforce with Zoho CRM Plus, saved ~$200K/year
- A manufacturer hit ~610% ROI on Zoho One by retiring legacy apps
